Footage keeps its audio: it plays in preview and export writes it. On top of that,
two audio features are built today — waveforms and beat detection.

<Shot file="waveform.png" caption="A footage layer's waveform in its lane." />

## Waveforms

A Footage layer draws its audio waveform in its Timeline lane, trimmed and retimed
with the layer.

:::note[Partly built]
Clips inside a [Sequence layer](/use/sequence-layers/) do not draw waveforms yet.
:::

## Beat markers

Lumit can read a composition's audio and place a **beat marker** on each detected
onset, to cut and animate against.

- **Composition ▸ Detect beats** runs the detection.
- **Composition ▸ Clear beat markers** removes what it placed.

Sensitivity tuning and tapping beats in by hand are not built yet. See
[Markers](/use/markers/).

## What is still to come

| Feature | What it will do |
| --- | --- |
| **Audio layer** | A layer whose source is an audio item, or the audio of a footage item. |
| **Audio panel** | Level meters and mixing controls. |
| **Mixing** | Several audio layers per composition, mixed together. |

The audio clock is intended to be the master everything else syncs to.
